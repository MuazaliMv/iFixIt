## 1. Purpose

This appendix records only the launch-promotion details from the supplied Provider Subscription Promotional Strategy that were not already explicit in `STEP_14_PROVIDER_SUBSCRIPTION_LAUNCH_PROMOTION.md`.

Step 14 remains authoritative for campaign eligibility, pricing stages, billing calculation, campaign data model, founding-provider entitlement rules, admin controls, reporting, audit and unresolved billing decisions.

This appendix adds:
- provider-facing promotional CTAs and acknowledgement flow
- billing schedule navigation/actions
- exact example campaign message templates

- illustrative acquisition/revenue forecast values from the source strategy

It does not change any existing approved subscription or MVP rule.

## 2. Promotional Registration UX

When a provider qualifies for the launch campaign, onboarding may display a dedicated promotional-pricing screen before activation.

The screen should support:
- registration/qualification date
- current promotional stage
- standard Professional Plan price
- current payable amount
- future campaign pricing stages
- standard post-promotion price
- estimated savings based on the authoritative configured billing interpretation
- explicit acknowledgement that future prices will increase according to the campaign schedule

Recommended actions:
- `I understand the pricing`
- `Start Free Trial` when current payable amount is MVR 0
- `Claim Promotion` / `Continue` for paid promotional stages

The user must not be shown a savings figure that is inconsistent with the actual configured billing periods.

## 4. Provider Subscription Dashboard UX Additions

In addition to the Step 14 dashboard requirements, the provider-facing subscription area should support direct actions for:
- `View Full Billing Schedule`
- `Update Payment Method` when the selected payment integration supports stored/replaceable payment methods
- `Renew Now`
- `Upgrade`
- `Cancel` subject to subscription policy

The billing timeline should visually distinguish:
- completed/paid periods
- current active promotional period
- upcoming promotional periods
- first standard-price renewal

The screen may show promotional savings, but only from actual price-schedule records.

## 5. Annual-Plan Upsell

The source strategy includes a standard-pricing message encouraging the provider to lock in value with a 12-month plan.

The system should therefore allow a configurable annual-plan upsell after or during the promotion, where a valid annual subscription product exists.

Requirements:
- annual price/discount must come from subscription-plan configuration
- no implied annual discount unless one is actually configured
- switching from promotion to annual plan must follow explicit proration/stacking rules
- any lifetime Founding Provider discount interaction must be resolved before purchase

Exact annual pricing remains governed by the subscription product configuration and Step 14 discount rules.

## 6. Founding Provider Badge Sharing

Qualifying Founding Providers may be offered a `Share Founding Provider Badge` action.

Potential share targets:
- system share sheet on mobile/PWA
- copy share link
- downloadable/shareable profile card generated by the application where implemented

The shared asset must:
- identify the provider as an early iFixIt platform member only
- not imply government endorsement or professional certification
- not expose private provider data
- link to the provider's public profile where appropriate

This is a marketing feature and may be deferred if it adds unnecessary MVP complexity.

## 8. Scarcity / Remaining-Spots UI

The source promotional mockups include messages such as `Only 10 spots left`.

This may only be displayed when:
- the campaign has a real configured provider limit; and
- the remaining count is calculated from server-side campaign enrollment data.

If no real cap exists, the UI must not show a remaining-spots message.

## 11. Launch Stage Business Objectives

The source strategy assigns a commercial objective to each promotional stage. These objectives are now preserved as campaign-planning metadata rather than billing logic.

| Stage | Commercial Objective |
|---|---|
| Days 1–15 / MVR 0 | Build the initial provider base as quickly as possible and reduce signup friction. |
| Days 16–30 / MVR 30 | Maintain provider-acquisition momentum while introducing the first paid conversion. |
| Days 31–60 / MVR 150 | Transition the provider base toward a normal paid-subscription habit while retaining a strong incentive. |
| Days 61–90 / MVR 225 | Provide the final discounted transition before standard pricing. |
| Day 91+ / MVR 299 | Operate the Professional plan at sustainable recurring standard pricing. |

Requirements:
- objectives may be stored with campaign/stage planning configuration or analytics metadata
- objectives do not alter eligibility, billing, verification or provider rights
- the admin reporting layer may compare stage outcomes with these objectives
- commercial objectives may be edited for future campaigns without altering historical billing records

## 12. Campaign Goal Framework

The launch strategy is intended to achieve four primary business outcomes:

1. rapid provider acquisition during launch
2. gradual transition from free/heavily discounted access to paid subscription
3. early-adopter retention through Founding Provider recognition and configured lifetime discounts
4. sustainable recurring provider-subscription revenue after the promotional period

The platform should therefore be able to report, where data is available:
- registrations and verified activations by campaign stage
- conversion from free to first paid stage
- conversion between each paid promotional stage
- conversion into standard-price subscription
- retention of Founding Providers versus non-founding providers
- campaign churn by stage
- subscription revenue by campaign cohort
- effect of lifetime discounts on recurring revenue

These are management/reporting requirements only and must not change transactional subscription truth.

## 13. Acceptance Criteria Additions

1. A qualifying provider sees a campaign pricing acknowledgement screen before activation where required.
2. The screen shows current and future charges from campaign price-schedule data.
3. `Start Free Trial` is shown only when the current authoritative charge is zero.
4. Provider can open the full billing schedule from the subscription dashboard.
5. Payment-method update is shown only when supported by the configured payment method/provider.
6. Stage-specific CTA wording may change without a code deployment.
7. Founding Provider badge sharing does not expose private information or imply official certification.
8. Annual-plan upsell cannot display a discount that is not configured.
9. Notification templates use actual provider-specific prices and effective dates.
10. Remaining-spots messaging cannot appear unless backed by real campaign capacity data.
11. Financial forecasts remain separate from transactional subscription data.
12. Actual campaign metrics can be compared with planning targets without changing billing behavior.
13. Each launch stage can carry a configurable commercial objective for reporting/planning purposes.
14. Campaign reports can measure free-to-paid, stage-to-stage and standard-price conversion without changing subscription state.
