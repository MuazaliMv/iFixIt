iFixIt / FixIt — GENERAL CUSTOMER USE CASES
================================================

Scope: UC-C01 to UC-C44

Important architecture rules:
- UC-C29 is OPTIONAL. FixIt does not collect, hold, route, settle, or refund customer-to-provider payments.
- Customer pays provider directly outside FixIt.
- UC-C41 KYC is OPTIONAL BY DEFAULT and is required only by separately approved risk/service/organization/legal rules.
- Provider ACCEPT does not equal assignment. Customer selects the final provider.
- Core request lifecycle: PENDING -> RESPONDED -> ACCEPTED -> INSPECTION_SCHEDULED -> IN_PROGRESS -> COMPLETED; alternative terminal status CANCELLED.
